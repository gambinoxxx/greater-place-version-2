Four ways organizations get involved.

From a one-time gift-in-kind to a standing sponsorship, here's what partnership can look like.

01

Corporate Sponsorship

02

School Partnerships

Bring training, workshops, or a pipeline into enrollment directly to your students.

03

In-Kind Support

Co-host events, cross-promote programs, or refer families into a shared pipeline of support.
